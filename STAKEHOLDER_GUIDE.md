# EDMS stakeholder guide

Open the local app with a fictional Records Manager identity. Upload a fictional file, review scan suggestions, select its document type and department, add a business reference and tags, and confirm metadata. Search its title, reference and filename; enable saved-text search when the scan produced an index. Open details, preview supported content and download under an authorised role. Update metadata, explicitly link a related document and upload a minor or major version. Check that old versions remain accessible.

Browse department/type views, My Uploads and metadata-driven shared collections. Configure an additional type or collection, and set mandatory fields for that type. Archive/removal rules are disabled until a fictional organisational rule reference is entered. Removal is soft and retains files and history.

Enter as Auditor to verify read-only metadata access and denied downloads/restricted documents. Enter as System Administrator to inspect roles and configuration while business documents remain inaccessible. Evaluation role selection is not real organisational sign-in.

Business cases and approvals remain the responsibility of existing systems. The repository's business reference has no live external integration. Permanent storage, real identity and the Supabase migration/file broker remain integration work, not deployed features.
